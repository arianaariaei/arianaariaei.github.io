About Me

Welcome! I am a Computer Engineering student at Babol Noshirvani University of Technology, passionate about the intersection of artificial intelligence and medicine. My research focuses on developing AI applications for healthcare, with a particular emphasis on cardiology.

My interest in cardiology stems from my childhood, growing up with my mother who is a cardiologist. Observing her work sparked my curiosity about the heart and cardiovascular diseases, and now I am combining this fascination with my technical expertise in machine learning and deep learning. I believe AI has tremendous potential to revolutionize cardiac diagnosis and treatment, and I am committed to contributing to this exciting field through my research and projects.
